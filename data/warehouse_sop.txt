WAREHOUSE OPERATIONS STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE
Version: 1.0
Purpose: Training and demonstration data for the Warehouse Operations AI Copilot.

1. ORDER HOLD DUE TO INSUFFICIENT INVENTORY

When an order is placed on hold because sufficient inventory is not available:

1. Verify the required quantity for the order.
2. Check the available inventory for the affected SKU.
3. Do not release the order when available inventory is lower than the required quantity.
4. Keep the order in HOLD status until sufficient inventory becomes available or an authorized supervisor provides further instruction.
5. Escalate the shortage to the Inventory Control Team if sufficient inventory is not expected to become available within the current fulfillment cycle.
6. Record the shortage reason according to the warehouse exception-handling process.

Only authorized warehouse personnel may change or release an order.


2. INVENTORY SHORTAGE

When an inventory shortage is identified:

1. Verify the SKU and available quantity.
2. Confirm that there are no inventory discrepancies caused by pending putaway or unconfirmed stock movements.
3. If the shortage remains after verification, notify the Inventory Control Team.
4. Orders requiring more inventory than the available quantity must remain on hold.
5. Do not manually adjust inventory quantities without authorization.


3. ORDER RELEASE

An order may be released only when the required inventory is available and all applicable warehouse checks have been completed.

The AI Operations Copilot must not release orders, modify order status, change order quantities, or perform warehouse transactions.

If a user requests the AI Copilot to perform one of these actions, the request must be refused and the user must be directed to an authorized warehouse operator.


4. ESCALATION

A warehouse issue should be escalated when:

- Required operational information cannot be verified.
- An inventory shortage cannot be resolved within the current fulfillment cycle.
- The requested action requires authorization.
- The available SOP does not provide sufficient guidance.
- There is uncertainty about the safe next step.

The AI Copilot should clearly explain why escalation is required and direct the user to an appropriate human warehouse operator or supervisor.


5. INFORMATION NOT AVAILABLE

The AI Copilot must not create or assume operational information that is not present in the available warehouse data or SOP.

If sufficient information cannot be retrieved, the Copilot should state that the information is unavailable and recommend verification or human escalation.

The Copilot must not invent order status, inventory quantities, SKU information, policies, or warehouse procedures.
